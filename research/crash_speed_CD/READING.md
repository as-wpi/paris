# Reading (written after the run; rule frozen before it)

**All five arms RETIRE.** The two-speed sizer does what it was built to do — cut faster in crashes — but by too little and at too much cost. With the λ=0.85 fast leg the fast-crash median drawdown improves by 0.7 points against a 2-point bar; with λ=0.80, 1.2 points. Both lose Sharpe (−0.016 and −0.025, CIs excluding zero), both raise turnover past the 1.25× bar (1.37× and 1.60×), and both fail the calm-control bleed in 2021, the year of the meme-stock and inflation volatility bursts inside a bull market: the fast leg keeps de-sizing on spikes that never became declines. The shock rule alone is a wash (+0.0 points, +0.004 Sharpe, turnover 1.23×) because three-sigma days in these vehicles mostly arrive *after* the sizer has already cut. Stacking the shock rule on the two-speed sizer adds nothing the two-speed sizer did not already do.

**Why the bound was not reachable.** Test A's sizer-only k=5 ceiling was 25% of fast-crash MaxDD, about 2 points at these depths. A causal fast filter reaches a third to a half of that ceiling, and the remainder is exactly the part that only foreknowledge could buy: the first two or three days of a crash, when the fast EWMA has not yet seen enough large returns to move. The price of the part it does reach is paid every time volatility spikes without a crash — 2021 is the clean example — which is the Cederburg et al. objection made concrete: real-time vol management gains what it gains in crashes and gives it back on false alarms.

**Disposition.** The incumbent EWMA λ=0.94 sizer stays the sizer of record. The sizer side of the crash-speed question is closed on this evidence: measurement (four estimators) and speed (two decays and a jump rule) have both been tested and both retire. Per the pre-registered order, the switch-side arms E (circuit breaker), F (asymmetric penalty) and G (turbulence exit) are next, then H (drawdown governor). Given that the switch-only k=5 ceiling was only 16% and the switch's whole edge is its low flip rate, the prior for E–G is below one in five each; they are worth running mainly to close the question cleanly.
