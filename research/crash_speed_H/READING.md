# Reading (written after the run; rule frozen before it; the scale control is POST HOC and labelled)

**By the letter, H_parity passes both verdicts; in substance it is the incumbent at half size.** The screener-parity governor (α=0.60, m=1.25) holds a mean weight of 0.21 against the incumbent's 0.43, and its full-sample numbers — Sharpe 0.43, Martin 1.05, MaxDD −12.8%, fast-crash MaxDD −4.0%, CAGR 4.4% — are matched to the second decimal by the incumbent scaled by a constant 0.5 (post-hoc control, `posthoc_scale_control.csv`): Sharpe 0.43, Martin 1.07, MaxDD −13.6%, fast-crash −4.3%, CAGR 4.5%. The governor's dynamic component adds nothing that a fixed half position does not: the trailing-peak floor never binds hard enough inside these 6–12-point episodes to move away from its structural m(1−α) = 0.5 exposure. The registered rule lacked a constant-scale control and so scored size as governance — the same participation artefact the frozen-rule benchmark exposed in the morning, now in the sizing layer.

**H_full is the honest test of governor value, and it retires.** With full exposure at highs and the same 40% budget, the floor binds only after a 20%+ drawdown from the trailing peak, which these episodes never reach on the sized stack; fast-crash MaxDD improves 0.3 points, Sharpe falls 0.026 (CI touching zero), Martin −0.03, turnover ×1.26. A governor with a budget wide enough not to cost exposure at highs is a governor that does not act.

**Disposition.** Neither arm is adopted. H_parity's pass is recorded as scale, not a crash-speed or governor result; a smaller vol target is the honest way to buy that risk profile and is a portfolio decision, not a rule. **Process fix, adopted:** every sizing or governor pre-registration from now on includes a weight-matched constant-scale control arm, and the verdict is taken against that control, not against the unscaled incumbent.

**The crash-speed question is closed.** A through H: the attribution bound said 41% of fast-crash drawdown was reachable with five days of foreknowledge; no causal rule on either the switch or the sizer reached a material fraction of it without paying more in Sharpe, bleed or turnover than it saved, and the one arm that "passed" did so by holding less. The first two or three days of a crash are silent to any daily close-to-close filter. The stack stays: trend λ5 rolling switch, EWMA λ=0.94 vol-target, same-close execution.
